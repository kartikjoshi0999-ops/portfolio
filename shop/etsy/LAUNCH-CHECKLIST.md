# Launch checklist

Nine listings, one shop, one afternoon. Tick as you go.

## Before you open Etsy

- [ ] Download the whole `shop/dist/` folder to the computer you will list from
- [ ] Open each of the six spreadsheets once yourself, just to see what a buyer sees
- [ ] Open both PDFs and check they render on your machine
- [ ] Decide your shop name from the list in `00-SHOP-SETUP.md` and check it is available

> **On the spreadsheets.** Every workbook is set to recalculate on open, so a buyer sees a fully
> populated template the moment they open it — in Excel, Google Sheets, Numbers or LibreOffice.
> Opening each one yourself before you list it is still worth two minutes: you will answer buyer
> questions better if you have actually clicked around the file you are selling.

## Shop setup (about 45 minutes)

- [ ] Create the shop: language English, country Canada, currency **CAD**
- [ ] Shop name set
- [ ] Shop icon uploaded — `shop/dist/images/00-shop/shop-icon-500x500.png`
- [ ] Big banner uploaded — `shop/dist/images/00-shop/shop-banner-1600x400.png`
- [ ] Announcement pasted from `00-SHOP-SETUP.md` §3
- [ ] About story and shop member bio pasted from §4
- [ ] Five shop sections created per §5
- [ ] All policies and eight FAQs entered per §6
- [ ] Message to buyers pasted from §7
- [ ] Etsy Payments enabled, bank account added, identity verification submitted
- [ ] Etsy Ads left **off**

## The nine listings (about 10 minutes each)

For each numbered file in this folder, in order:

- [ ] 01 — Canadian Budget & Cash-Flow Tracker — **$9.99**
- [ ] 02 — TFSA vs RRSP Contribution Planner — **$8.99**
- [ ] 03 — Debt Payoff Planner — **$7.99**
- [ ] 04 — Investment Portfolio Tracker — **$12.99**
- [ ] 05 — Small Business 12-Month Cash Flow Forecast — **$16.99**
- [ ] 06 — 3-Statement Financial Model Template — **$27.99**
- [ ] 07 — Newcomer to Canada Money Starter Kit — **$7.99**
- [ ] 08 — Finance & Banking Interview Prep Pack — **$11.99**
- [ ] 09 — The Complete Finance Bundle — **$49.99** ← set as featured listing

For every one: title, description, 13 tags, price, quantity 999, **type: Digital**, the four images
in order, and the digital file(s). All of it is in the listing's own markdown file.

## The day you go live

- [ ] Set listing 09 (the bundle) as the shop's featured listing
- [ ] Add the shop link to the Shop section of your portfolio site (`index.html`) and to `shop.html`
- [ ] Add the shop link to your LinkedIn featured section
- [ ] Buy one of your own listings from a second account if you have one, to confirm the buyer
      experience end to end — the download email, the file, the message to buyers

## First 30 days

- [ ] Do not discount anything. Full price until 10 sales and 5 reviews.
- [ ] Message every buyer once, a week after purchase, asking whether the file worked for them.
      Do not ask for a review directly in that message — ask whether it worked. The reviews follow.
- [ ] Post one of the four listing images per product on LinkedIn, spread over the month, with a short
      note about the problem the template solves. You already have a professional audience there.
- [ ] After 10 sales: turn on a 15% coupon for people who favourite the shop
- [ ] After 20 sales: consider Etsy Ads at $1–3 per day, on the bundle and the 3-statement model only

## What to watch, and what to do about it

| Signal | What it means | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| Views but no favourites | The main image is not landing | Swap image 1 for image 2 on that listing |
| Favourites but no sales | Price or trust gap | Leave the price alone until you have reviews; strengthen the description's WHO IT IS FOR section |
| No views at all after 2 weeks | Tags are not matching search | Rewrite the weakest 4 tags with longer, more specific phrases |
| Sales on one listing only | You have found the product that works | Build the next product adjacent to it, not somewhere new |
