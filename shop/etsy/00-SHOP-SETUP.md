## 6. Policies

Etsy asks you to build a policy set. Here is each answer.

### Returns and exchanges

Select **"I don't accept returns or exchanges"**, which is the standard and correct setting for
instant digital downloads. Then paste this into the additional details box:

```
Every item in this shop is an instant digital download, so it cannot be returned once it has been
delivered.

That is Etsy's rule for digital goods, not an attempt to avoid standing behind the work. If a file
will not open, arrives corrupted, or is genuinely not what the listing described, message me and I
will fix it or refund you. Tell me what happened and I will sort it out.
```

### Cancellations

Accept cancellations **only if the buyer has not yet downloaded the file**, and say so:

```
I can cancel an order if you message me before you download the file. Once the file has been
downloaded it cannot be returned.
```

### Privacy

```
I only ever see the information Etsy shares with me to complete your order. I do not collect anything
else, I do not use your details for marketing, and I do not share them with anyone.
```

### FAQs — add all eight

**Will this work in Google Sheets?**
```
Yes. Upload the .xlsx to Google Drive and open it with Google Sheets, and every formula carries over.
Two things behave slightly differently in Sheets: the drop-down lists may need to be re-applied, and
chart styling can shift a little. The calculations themselves are unaffected.
```

**Do I need to be good at Excel?**
```
No. Every file has a Start Here tab that tells you exactly what to do, and the cells are colour-coded:
yellow means type here, grey means a formula lives there and you should leave it alone. If you can
type a number into a box, you can use these.
```

**Is this a subscription?**
```
No. You buy the file once and it is yours. There is nothing to log in to, nothing to renew, and it
keeps working offline.
```

**Can I use it for my business?**
```
Yes. The licence covers your own personal or business use, including editing it however you like.
What you cannot do is resell it, share it, or include it in a product of your own.
```

**Is this financial advice?**
```
No. These are educational planning tools built by a finance professional, but they are not advice and
buying one does not create an advisor relationship. Contribution limits and tax rules change — check
current figures with the CRA or a licensed advisor before you act on anything a template tells you.
```

**Are the Canadian numbers up to date?**
```
The TFSA limits run from 2009 through 2026 and the RRSP dollar limits through 2026, with a note on
each tab telling you to confirm the current year's figure with the CRA. Limits are indexed annually,
so the file tells you where to check rather than pretending it will never go stale.
```

**Can you customise a template for me?**
```
Often, yes. Message me with what you need and I will tell you honestly whether it is a small change,
a custom build, or something you would be better off doing another way.
```

**How fast do you reply?**

```
Usually the same day, and always within 24 hours. I answer my own messages.
```

## 8. Payment, tax and admin

- **Payment**: enable Etsy Payments, add your Canadian bank account, and complete identity verification.
  Etsy will hold your first payout until this is done — do it on day one, not on your first sale.
- **Deposit schedule**: weekly is fine to start.
- **Sales tax**: for digital sales to Canadian buyers, Etsy generally collects and remits sales tax
  itself. Whether **you** need to register for GST/HST depends on your own revenue — the CRA small
  supplier threshold is $30,000 in a rolling four-quarter period. **Confirm your position with the CRA
  or an accountant before you assume anything**; this document is not tax advice.
- **Bookkeeping**: Etsy's own CSV export plus one spreadsheet is enough at this volume. You already own
  a suitable one.

## 9. Etsy Ads — leave them off at launch

Do not turn on Etsy Ads until you have at least 10 organic sales. Paid traffic on listings with no
reviews converts badly and you will conclude the products do not sell when the real problem is the
absence of social proof.

Offsite Ads are compulsory once a shop passes US$10,000 in sales over 12 months, and optional below
that. The fee only applies when a buyer actually arrives through an Etsy-placed ad, and the net-of-fee
figures in PRICING-AND-FEES.md show both cases.
