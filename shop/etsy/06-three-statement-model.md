Most spreadsheets sold as financial models are three separate tables that never tie. This one ties, and it tells you when it does not — two integrity checks sit on the dashboard where you can see them.

WHAT YOU GET
✓ Three statements genuinely linked — net income to retained earnings, closing cash to the balance sheet cash line
✓ Working capital driven by DSO, DIO and DPO, the way analysts model it, not as a flat percentage
✓ PP&E roll-forward with capex and depreciation, and a retained earnings roll with dividends
✓ No circular references and no iterative calculation to switch on — interest is charged on opening debt
✓ A balance check on every year that says BALANCED or flags the exact amount it is out by
✓ A second check confirming closing cash ties to the balance sheet
✓ 13 ratios: margins, current and quick, debt/equity, debt/EBITDA, interest coverage, ROE, ROA, cash conversion cycle and free cash flow
